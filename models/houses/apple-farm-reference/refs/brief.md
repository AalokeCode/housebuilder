### Measurement basis
The published floor area is a project total, not verified carpet area. The earlier 100 m2 client request is a design brief, not the final area. Do not add the deck or canopy to the floor-area total without a stated measurement standard.

### Vendor information requested
Obtain a measured plan, building register, cadastral/title documents, service records and a condition disclosure. Confirm access rights, land included in a sale, heating system, water/waste arrangements and the legal status of the outdoor work area.

## Floor plan and spatial sequence

Plan legend: 1 Entrance; 2 Utility; 3 Kitchen; 4 Living; 5 Guestroom; 6 Bathroom; 7 Masterroom; 8 Storage; 9 Boilerroom. The connecting corridor is not separately numbered.

### Read the plan before matching photos
Entry is at the right-hand end beside the covered exterior area. Kitchen/dining and living form the shared zone; the corridor serves the guestroom, bathroom and masterroom. Storage and boiler spaces project from the back, while the deck opens from the living area.

### Scale discrepancy - retain for review
The printed 0-10 m bar appears inconsistent with the reported 123.23 m2 area: a literal image reading suggests an enclosed extent around 39 x 10 m. This rough check is not a measured footprint. Room dimensions remain blank until a reliable drawing is supplied.

### Photographer follow-up
Request doorway-to-corner views of both bedrooms and the bathroom, reverse views of every opening, one uninterrupted corridor walkthrough, and a tape/laser ceiling-height record. Request original photo files and capture dates.
